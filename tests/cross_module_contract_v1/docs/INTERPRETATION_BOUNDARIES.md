# Interpretation Boundaries

- Youth Score is not chronological biological age.
- Young-Old separation is not a validated continuous aging clock.
- Droplet transfer is cross-assay sensitivity, not independent external validation.
- GSE176206 has n=3 animals per age group and is descriptive evidence.
- n=3 does not by itself prove training overfitting because models were frozen outside GSE; tuning after viewing GSE results would introduce circularity.
- `state_remodeling_dominant` is not accelerated aging.
- Negative Youth Score is not proof of harm.
- Risk axes are not clinical safety or tumour diagnoses; Genomic Stress is not direct cancer measurement; cell-cycle activity is not tumour risk.
- Kaile contextual Risk is not Kei final Risk. Pseudotime is not experimental time. Canonical endogenous pluripotency markers are not source reprogramming-state labels.
- Geneformer fixed-length improvement is not full axis validation; old TF ranking remains uninterpretable.
- Current results do not establish a validated Safe Zone.
