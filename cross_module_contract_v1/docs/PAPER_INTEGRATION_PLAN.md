The two Youth models remain independent frozen scorers. Raw score magnitude is not compared, no winner is selected, and no ensemble is built. The sensitivity comparison tests whether conclusions depend on one Youth definition. The GSE two-model follow-up shows concordant SOKM direction while retaining different estimands; the Kaile state-remodeling package is a separate multi-axis decomposition. The active GSE interpretation is `state_remodeling_dominant`.

Kaile contextual Risk is not Kei final Risk. Mashiro Risk remains preliminary pending CSV, script, pairing, and threshold confirmation. Geneformer is a characterised methodological negative: fixed length partially clarifies mechanism but does not restore candidate ranking, and donor pseudobulk separation is only 7/14. The paper must not claim a validated Safe Zone.
