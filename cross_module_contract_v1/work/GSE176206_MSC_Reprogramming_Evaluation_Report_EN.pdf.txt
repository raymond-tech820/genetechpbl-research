Abstract
Objective: To explain why a frozen Youth Score model trained on cleaned TMS FACS Limb Muscle MSCs
assigns lower scores to aged SOKM cells in the external GSE176206 MSC dataset, and to distinguish natural-
age signals from MSC identity loss, cell-cycle changes, and reprogramming-associated state transitions.
Methods: The analysis used raw cell-by-gene counts from GSE176206 (20,661 cells and 28,694 genes)
without refitting the Youth Scoremodel on the external data. Counts were total-count normalized to 10,000 per
cell and log(1+x) transformed for module scoring. MSC identity, ECM/collagen, E2F, G2M, reprogramming,
SenMayo, p53, and DNA-repair modules were frozen before effect inspection. Each module was evaluated
by two methods: (1) an expression-matched, 100-control-set background-corrected module score; and (2) a
within-cell expression-rank score. An axis was called robust only when both methods agreed in direction.
Three mice with known animal identifiers formed the paired inferential unit. Cells with unknown animal
identifiers were retained only for descriptive sensitivity analysis.
Results: Among aged known animals, the donor-paired median-cell effects for SOKM − control were:
Youth Score−0.1786; MSC identity −0.0809 by the primary method and−0.1408 by rank; and ECM −0.2161
by the primary method and −0.1539 by rank. The baseline/Col11a1-high state fraction decreased by 0.6573,
whereas reprogramming and reprogramming-cycling increased by 0.5728 and 0.1732, respectively; ordinary
cycling decreased by 0.0887. The combined cell-cycle axis increased by +0.0412 under the primary method
but decreased by −0.0196 under the rank method. Mean-cell decomposition of the Youth Score shift yielded
a shared-state within component of −0.0522, a shared-state composition component of −0.2395, and a non-
overlap residual of +0.1432.
Conclusion: The best-supported interpretation is that SOKM causes substantial cell-state remodeling and
loss of MSC/ECM identity together with a negativeYouth Scoreshift. The evidence does not support a uniform
increase in proliferation across the entire cell population, nor can the lower score be interpreted directly as
accelerated aging. Because only three paired mice were available, all intervals and permutation results are
descriptive.
Three boundaries for reading this report
• The Youth Score measures similarity to the TMS young Limb MSC transcriptional state; it is not
developmental time or true biological age.
• Velocity pseudotime is a source-provided trajectory coordinate; it is neither experimental time nor
longitudinal tracking of the same cell.
• Risk modules provide transcriptomic context and are not direct measurements of tumour formation,
toxicity, or clinical safety.
1 Research Question and Analytical Framework
1.1 Research question
In the external validation dataset, aged MSCs had a lower Youth Score after SOKM treatment than their
matched controls. This observation has at least three distinct possible explanations:
1. cells moved along a natural aging direction;
2. cells moved away from a “young MSC” identity even if that deviation was not equivalent to aging; or
3. the population composition changed, for example because many cells entered reprogramming or
reprogramming-cycling states, shifting the population transcriptome away from the young MSC
reference.
Accordingly, this report does not treat the Youth Score as a stand-alone answer. It jointly examines the

2.6 Unknown cells, cell states, and trajectory analyses
Unknown-cell sensitivity. Cells with unknown animal identifiers cannot be paired with controls and do
not increase the donor count. The workflow separately reports known-donor paired, known-cell-weighted,
unknown-cell-weighted, and all-cell-weighted effects to test whether unknown cells dominate the direction.
State mapping. Exact source-provided state annotations were retained, and four broad categories were cre-
ated for composition analysis:
• baseline_or_col11a1: Dox+ baseline, Tg+ baseline, and Col11a1 high;
• cycling: the ordinary cycling state;
• reprogramming: reprogramming and reprogramming intermediate;
• reprogramming_cycling: states annotated as both reprogramming and cycling.
The baseline/Col11a1 grouping is a broad analytical category and does not automatically mean “normal” or

Why can reprogramming states increase while canonical pluripotency markers decrease?
The state labels are discrete annotations supplied by the GSE176206 authors and describe which transcrip-
tomic state a cell occupies. The frozen marker module is a continuous score derived from 13 canonical
endogenous mouse pluripotency genes. In the h5ad file, the exogenous four-factor construct is repre-
sented separately as Y4TF; the module features Pou5f1, Sox2, Klf4 , and Myc are endogenous genes
rather than transgene measurements. Consequently, SOKM can redistribute cells into source-labeled
reprogramming states without increasing this endogenous marker module. These measurements have
different definitions and neither can substitute for the other.
The E2F and G2M panels in Figure 2 show the primary background-corrected module scores and rise in
the aged paired comparison. However, the combined cell-cycle rank score changes slightly in the opposite di-
rection. The increase is therefore treated as method-sensitive and may reflect enrichment of a reprogramming-
cycling subpopulation rather than uniform cell-cycle activation across all MSCs.

4 Discussion
4.1 Best-supported biological interpretation
This analysis converted the proposed hypothesis that identity loss may obscure age-related signals into an
auditable quantitative test. Three lines of evidence were mutually consistent:
1. Continuous scores: the Youth Score, MSC identity, and ECM/collagen decreased together;
2. Discrete states: baseline/Col11a1-high states decreased substantially while reprogramming and
reprogramming-cycling states increased; and
3. Decomposition: shared-state composition was the largest negative component of the Youth Score shift,
with an additional negative within-state component.
The negative Youth Score shift therefore arose at least partly because cells moved away from the “young
Limb MSC” transcriptional state learned by the model. The score may respond not only to age-related expres-
sion but also to MSC identity, ECM, culture state, and reprogramming-induced changes orthogonal to natural
aging. The Youth Score alone cannot fully separate these mechanisms.
4.2 Why the data do not support robust global cell-cycle activation
State fractions showed an increase in reprogramming-cycling but a decrease in ordinary cycling. The primary
continuous module score showed a small increase, whereas the rank score showed a small decrease. Expansion
of a specific subpopulationand increased average cell-cycle activity acrossall cells are distinct questions. The
most accurate current statement is:
SOKM increased the fraction of the reprogramming-cycling substate, but two independent con-
tinuous scoring methods did not agree on the direction of global cell-cycle activity. Robust
population-wide cell-cycle activation is therefore not supported.
4.3 Relationship to Roux et al.
Roux et al. reported that partial-reprogramming effects are strongly dependent on cell identity. In muscle-
derived MSCs, dedifferentiation, cell-cycle programs, and other orthogonal remodeling can dominate changes
along the natural age axis. The present results are directionally consistent with that interpretation. However,
this analysis uses an independently frozen TMS Youth Score, literature/MSigDB modules, donor-paired statis-
tics, and known/unknown sensitivity analysis. It is therefore an independent quantification of the proposed
mechanism rather than a restatement of the source paper.

5.1 Principal limitations
1. Few donors: each age-by-condition group contained only three mice; all inferential results are descrip-
tive.
2. Unequal cell counts: animal 1 contributed most aged SOKM cells. Equal mouse weighting reduces but
cannot eliminate this limitation.
3. Unknown cells cannot be paired: the 1,975 unknown cells are useful for descriptive sensitivity analysis
but do not add biological replicates.
4. Modules are proxies: identity, cell cycle, SASP , p53, and DNA repair are transcriptional proxies rather
than direct functional measurements.
5. Method sensitivity: primary and rank methods disagreed for cell cycle, p53, and DNA repair; one
method cannot be selected post hoc as the sole conclusion.
6. States and pseudotime are source-derived: they are not experimental lineage tracing and do not estab-
lish the true destination of any individual cell.
7. Domain limitation of the Youth Score: the model was trained on TMS FACS Limb MSCs and may
respond jointly to age, identity, and assay/domain shift.
5.2 Priority next steps
1. Evaluate joint Y outh-identity-cycle trajectories separately within each mouse and exact source state to
avoid pooled-cell dominance.
2. Recheck the root, terminal state, and direction of the source velocity graph before assigning biological
direction to pseudotime.
3. Replicate identity/ECM and composition findings in additional independent MSC reprogramming
datasets or a cohort with more donors.
4. Validate the reprogramming-cycling fraction against EdU/Ki67, direct cell counts, or another functional
proliferation measurement.
5. Retain both primary and rank methods. If they disagree, report the result as method-sensitive rather than
selecting a method post hoc.
6 Conclusion
Under the frozen scoring framework and the current GSE176206 MSC dataset, aged SOKM cells did not move
toward the young Limb MSC state defined by TMS. More importantly, the negativeYouth Scoreshift coincided
with reduced MSC identity and ECM/collagen programs and with redistribution from baseline/Col11a1-high
states toward reprogramming and reprogramming-cycling states. The joint pattern identifiesreprogramming-
associated identity and state remodeling as the primary interpretation .
The present results do not support robust global cell-cycle activation and do not provide evidence suﬀi-
cient to interpret the transcriptomic changes as clinical safety, tumourigenicity, or accelerated aging. The
main achievement is a reproducible, donor-aware framework with explicit method-sensitivity controls. It ex-
plains why the Youth Score decreases while making clear that additional independent donors and functional
experiments are required to confirm mechanism.
