# Comparison-ready TMS Droplet cohorts

The file `donor_scores_all_16.csv` contains every available Droplet Limb MSC donor. For the model comparison requested by the team:

| Cohort | Inclusion | Donors | Donor-level Spearman rho (age vs Youth Score) |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Main 12-mouse cohort | 3, 18, 21, and 24 months | 12 | -0.873 |
| Old-only cohort | 18, 21, and 24 months | 10 | -0.817 |
| Full sensitivity cohort | 1, 3, 18, 21, 24, and 30 months | 16 | -0.856 |

The 1-month and 30-month mice are retained only for sensitivity analysis. The 3-month group contains two female mice; age and sex should be shown in any downstream scatter plot or analysis.
